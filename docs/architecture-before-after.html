Architecture Evolution

Before & After

A visual comparison of the old manual workflow and the new self-owned lead ingest pipeline. No code, no jargon — just the shape of the system.

Before — the old way

Four disconnected tools, manual glue, scattered data

Every lead used to bounce through four separate services. A human had to copy information between them by hand. Customer data lived in several places at once, and there was no single place to see the whole picture.

The old workflow: four separate tools, no connection between them, and a person manually moving data at every step.

VS
After — the new way

One pipeline, automatic steps, all data in one place

Now the visitor does everything in a single branded form. The system handles the waiver, signature, storage, mapping, and exports automatically. No copying, no re-typing, no scattered spreadsheets.

The new workflow: one form, then the system does the rest — storage, mapping, ticketing, and exports all happen automatically.

What actually improved

No more manual copying

The waiver, signature, and address are captured once in the form and stored directly. Nobody re-types anything.

One home for data

Leads, consent records, and signatures live together in one database instead of being scattered across forms, PDFs, and spreadsheets.

Automatic mapping

Addresses are geocoded and placed on a live map automatically — no more typing addresses into Google Earth by hand.

Instant exports

CSV, GeoJSON, and KML come straight from the dashboard with one click, ready for spreadsheets or Google Earth.

Built-in legal trail

Every signup records the exact waiver text, the typed signature, and a timestamp — automatically, every time.

Owned, not rented

The whole pipeline runs on infrastructure Benton Drones controls, rather than depending on four separate third-party tools.
