What actually improved

No more manual copying

The waiver, signature, and address are captured once in the form and stored directly. Nobody re-types anything.

One home for data

Leads, consent records, and signatures live together in one database instead of being scattered across forms, PDFs, and spreadsheets.

Automatic mapping

Addresses are geocoded and placed on a live map automatically — no more typing addresses into Google Earth by hand.

Instant exports

CSV, GeoJSON, and KML come straight from the dashboard with one click, ready for spreadsheets or Google Earth.

Built-in legal trail

Every signup records the exact waiver text, the typed signature, and a timestamp — automatically, every time.

Owned, not rented

The whole pipeline runs on infrastructure Benton Drones controls, rather than depending on four separate third-party tools.
